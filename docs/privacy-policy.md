# Sarvodhaya Hardware — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Sarvodhaya Hardware ("the app", "we") is a storefront for a hardware and building-supplies shop.
This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Account details** — name, email address and password when you register. Authentication is
  handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your password.
- **Contact and delivery details** — phone number and delivery address that you enter when
  placing an order. These are required to deliver goods.
- **Order information** — the products, quantities, prices, delivery fee and order status for
  each order you place.
- **Saved items** — your cart and wishlist.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

## How we use it

- To create and secure your account.
- To fulfil and deliver your orders and to show you your order history and status.
- To keep your cart and wishlist available across your devices.
- To respond to support requests you send us.

## Sharing

Order and contact details are shared only with the delivery partner that carries your order,
and only to the extent needed to deliver it. Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication
and Cloud Firestore). We do not sell your personal data.

## Retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. You can ask for your account and its
associated data (profile, cart, wishlist, order history) to be deleted by writing to the
support address below; deletion is completed within 30 days, except for records we must keep
for accounting or legal reasons.

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Firestore with rules that scope each user's cart,
wishlist and orders to that user's own account.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new
"last updated" date.
